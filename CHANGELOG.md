# Changelog

What changed in Jannoo for Mac, newest first.

## 1.0, build 12 · 27 October 2026

- Show or hide the sidebar with ⌃⌘S.
- Jump to boards with ⌘1 to ⌘9, or move around the board with new single keys.
- Mark Complete now uses ⇧⌘C instead of ⇧⌘D.

## 1.0, build 11 · 19 October 2026

- Fixed a crash that could affect builds 9 and 10.
- Tabs, list and outline views, and custom card fields are now part of Pro. Epics, card links and attachments stay free.
- Every download now includes 7 days of Pro.

## 1.0, build 10 · 19 October 2026

- Choose your background, card title size and focus glow in Settings ▸ Appearance, including the new near-black Midnight background.
- Open a board in a new tab with File ▸ New Tab (⌘T).
- Fixed two display problems around the tab bar.

## 1.0, build 9 · 16 October 2026

- The first public release of Jannoo 1.0.

## 1.0 beta, build 1 · September 2026

- Boards are folders of Markdown and JSON.
- Emoji reactions on cards, and your own emoji tags.
- The full-page card view: notes, checklist, properties and the GitHub comment thread.
- GitHub sync, with pull request badges on cards and a new board straight from a repo.
- The `jannoo` command line, and `jannoo agent install` for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor.
